Frequently asked questions

How is the winning chance calculated?

For each team we multiply the share of fans who support it by a likelihood built from its past win rate (win rate4 × (1 − win rate)3). The two results are divided by their total, so the chances add up to 100%. This is a simple Bayes rule.

Can I trust it as betting advice?

No. It is a simple, fun model. It ignores injuries, weather, pitch conditions and much more.

Does my support change the result?

Yes. Each support tap adds one fan to that team (once per match per visit), and the odds are recalculated.

How do I ask a question?

Open View details on any match and use the “Ask the admin” form at the bottom.

How do I get email updates?

Subscribe with your email at the bottom of the home page. You can only receive messages the admin sends.

Who can publish matches?

Only the admin, from the Admin panel after logging in.
